# Credits

This book is built from free and open tools. Each is listed here with its licence.

## Sheet music and playback

Music examples are drawn and played in your browser by [abcjs](https://github.com/paulrosen/abcjs), version 6.7.2, released under the MIT licence.

## Piano and guitar sounds

The piano and guitar sounds are a subset of the FluidR3_GM soundfont, as prepared in [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts). That repository's README says the files were "Generated from FluidR3_GM.sf2" and "Released under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license" ([CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/)).

## Font

The text is set in Jost, released under the SIL Open Font Licence and available from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jost).
