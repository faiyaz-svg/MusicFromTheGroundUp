# Free apps and gear

Besides your piano and guitar, you need a few small things. Most are free or cost little. App names, features and prices change, so the apps below were checked against their official store pages as of 2026; look at the store page before you install.

## For both instruments

A **metronome** is a device or app that clicks a steady beat. Your digital piano very likely has one built in; look for a Metronome or Tempo button, and check the manual. The site itself has no metronome. If you want a phone app, Tuner & Metronome by Soundcorset is free to install on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soundcorset.client.android) and [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id918055824), and it has a tuner too. It has paid extras; the basic click at a tempo you choose is free.

You also need:

- A notebook for the practice log described in [your daily practice](your-daily-practice.md). Any notebook works.
- A phone stand for filming yourself. The "Film yourself" boxes ask for a minute of video. A stand, a stack of books or a mug-propped phone all work.
- Anki, the flashcard app. As of 2026 it is free on desktop and on Android (where it is called AnkiDroid), and AnkiMobile on iPhone is paid. Get the desktop version from [apps.ankiweb.net](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) and check that page for the phone options. You do not need the phone app if you review on your computer.

## For the guitar

A tuner tells you whether each string is at the right pitch. A guitar drifts out of tune within days, and out-of-tune strings make correct fingering sound wrong. A clip-on tuner attaches to the headstock and reads the vibration, so it works in a noisy room. A phone app listens through the microphone. These install for free as of 2026:

| App | Android | iPhone |
|---|---|---|
| GuitarTuna (Yousician) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ovelin.guitartuna) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/guitartuna-tune-play-guitar/id527588389) |
| Fender Tune | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fender.tuner) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1107017950) |
| Tuner & Metronome (Soundcorset) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soundcorset.client.android) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id918055824) |

GuitarTuna also sells subscriptions for lessons; you only need the tuner.

Other guitar gear:

- A few picks. Medium gauge, around 0.6 to 0.73 mm thick, is a good start. They come in packs, and you will lose some.
- A capo, which is a clamp that holds all six strings down at one fret. You do not need one until the lesson on it in stage 5, so wait.
- A footstool or a guitar strap. Classical guitarists rest the guitar on the left leg with the left foot raised on a footstool. If you sit with the guitar on your right leg, or stand, use a strap. The posture lesson shows both.

## For the piano

Buy headphones whose plug fits your piano's headphone socket. Many pianos use a 6.35 mm socket, and a small adapter converts a 3.5 mm plug. Headphones let you play at any hour, and you can hear whether the volume is even from note to note.

## What you do not need yet

A teacher, a new instrument or any paid app. If a lesson needs something else, it says so at the top and names a free option where one exists.
