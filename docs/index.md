# Music From the Ground Up

A self-study music book for piano and guitar, for someone starting from zero. On the piano you learn to read sheet music and sight-read new pieces, up to classical pieces at about RCM Level 5 or 6. On the guitar you learn chords all over the neck, strumming and fingerpicking from chord charts and tab, and easy classical studies. Theory, ear training and improvising run through the whole book, with examples from classical music and from the chord moves behind film, game and anime music.

Every music example is drawn on the page and plays in your browser, with a tempo control so you can slow it down and play along. Each lesson is a short read followed by a 30-minute practice routine.

Start with [how to use this book](00-start-here/index.md), then work through the stages in order.
