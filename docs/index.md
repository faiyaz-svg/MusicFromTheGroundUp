# Music From the Ground Up

A self-study music book for piano and guitar, for someone starting from zero. It teaches you to read sheet music and sight-read on the piano, to play chords, strumming and fingerpicking from chord charts and tab on the guitar, and the theory that ties them together: scales, keys, chords, harmony, and the chord moves behind classical, film, game and anime music. Later stages add playing by ear and improvising.
