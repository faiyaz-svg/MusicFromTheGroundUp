# Chord ear quiz

A triad is a chord of three notes. The quiz plays a chord and you name its quality: major, minor, diminished or augmented. At level 3 it adds three seventh chords, which have a fourth note.

Choose "Block" to hear the notes together, as a pianist or strummer plays a chord, or "Broken" to hear them one by one. Broken is a good way to start, because you can pick out each note.

Press "Play again" until you are ready to answer. When you are wrong, the right button turns salmon and the tool lists the notes in the chord. Play them on the piano to feel the shape.

## Five minutes a day

1. Level 1 is major or minor. Major sounds bright and settled, and minor sounds darker.
2. Stay on level 1 until you get 9 of 10 right, then add diminished and augmented.
3. Answer 20 questions, then play the chords you missed on your instrument.
4. Try a few questions with your hand on the keys, so the sound and the shape connect.

Diminished sounds tense and compressed. Augmented sounds open and unresolved.
