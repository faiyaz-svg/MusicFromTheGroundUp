# Fretboard drill

The fretboard drill trains where the notes are on your guitar. The picture shows the strings as they look in tab: string 1, the thinnest, is on top, and string 6 is on the bottom. Frets 0 to 12 run left to right, and fret 0 is the open string. The strings are tuned E2 A2 D3 G3 B3 E4.

There are two modes. In "Name the note" a dot appears and you tap its letter. In "Find the note" you get a note name and tap every place it appears in the area you chose, and each right answer turns teal.

Start with strings 6 and 5 only. Add the next string when you know those two without hesitating.

## Five minutes a day

1. Use "Name the note" on strings 6 and 5, frets 0 to 5, for two minutes. Aim for under 5 seconds per note.
2. Switch to "Find the note" for two minutes. Say each note aloud as you tap it.
3. Spend the last minute on the guitar. Play the notes you missed.

Leave "Include sharps" off until the natural notes are easy. A sharp is the note between two others, such as F# between F and G.
