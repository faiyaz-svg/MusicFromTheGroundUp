# Practice tools

These five tools run in your browser on a phone or laptop, so you can keep them next to the piano or guitar. Each takes about 5 minutes. Use one a day after your warm-up, and rotate through them over the week.

Your flashcard progress and your settings are saved on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. If your browser blocks storage, the tools still work, but they forget your settings when you leave the page.

| Tool | What it trains |
|---|---|
| [Note flashcards](note-flashcards.md) | Knowing which note is which on the staff, and which key it is on the piano. Start here. |
| [Reading drill](reading-drill.md) | Reading a new four-bar melody, then checking it by ear. |
| [Fretboard drill](fretboard-drill.md) | Knowing the notes along the guitar neck. |
| [Interval ear quiz](interval-quiz.md) | Hearing the distance between two notes. |
| [Chord ear quiz](chord-quiz.md) | Hearing whether a chord is major, minor or something else. |

Sound starts only when you tap a button, because browsers block sound that starts by itself. If you hear nothing on a phone, turn up the volume and check the silent switch.
