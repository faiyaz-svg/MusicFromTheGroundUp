# Interval ear quiz

An interval is the distance between two notes. The quiz plays two notes and you name the interval, such as a major 3rd or a perfect 5th. Each one has a sound of its own, and you learn it by hearing it many times.

Choose how the notes play. "Going up" and "Going down" play one note after the other. "Together" plays both at once, which is harder at first. Press "Play again" as often as you like before you answer.

When you miss, the right button turns salmon and the tool names the two notes. Play them on your instrument, then press "Next question".

## Five minutes a day

1. Stay on level 1 until you get 9 of 10 right.
2. Do about 20 questions, with "Going up" for the first minutes.
3. Hum the second note before you answer.
4. Move up a level only when the current one feels calm.

Tie each interval to a tune you know well. Listen for the width: a 2nd is a small step, a 5th is a wide, open sound, and an octave sounds like the same note, higher.
