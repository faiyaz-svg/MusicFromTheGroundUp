# Note flashcards

A note appears on a staff and you name it. Knowing the notes without counting lines is the base skill of reading music, and it only comes from many short, quick repeats. This tool does the repeating for you.

Choose one or more card sets, then pick how you answer. With letter buttons you name the note (A to G, or press the key on a laptop). With the piano keyboard you tap the exact key, so the octave counts too. Use the keyboard answer once you know the letters.

The tool keeps five boxes. A card you get right moves up a box and shows less often. A card you miss goes back to box 1 and comes back within a few cards. That way your time goes to the notes you do not know yet.

## Five minutes a day

1. Start with "Treble: middle C to G" and answer by letter.
2. Aim for under 3 seconds per card. If the average is higher, slow down and add no new sets.
3. When nearly every card is in box 4 or 5, tick the next set.
4. Say each wrong answer aloud, with its octave, before you tap Next.

Turn on "Play the note after each answer" to connect the sound to the symbol.
