# Glossary

## A

- **Action**: the mechanism under the keys that sets their weight and feel. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))
- **Arm weight**: using the weight of the loose arm to press a key. ([Sitting at the piano and finger numbers](../01-first-sounds/05-sitting-and-finger-numbers.md))

## B

- **Bar** (also called a measure): a group of beats between two bar lines. ([Bars, time signatures and rests](../01-first-sounds/08-bars-time-signatures-and-rests.md))
- **Bar line**: the vertical line that marks the end of a bar. ([Bars, time signatures and rests](../01-first-sounds/08-bars-time-signatures-and-rests.md))
- **Beat**: the steady pulse of the music. ([Beat, tempo and note values](../01-first-sounds/07-beat-tempo-and-note-values.md))
- **Beats per minute (bpm)**: the unit for tempo; 60 bpm is one click per second. ([How to practise](../01-first-sounds/01-how-to-practise.md), [Beat, tempo and note values](../01-first-sounds/07-beat-tempo-and-note-values.md))
- **Body**: the hollow part of the guitar. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Bridge**: the part of the top that anchors the strings. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))

## C

- **Chord**: three or more notes played together. ([Chord diagrams and your first chord](../01-first-sounds/12-chord-diagrams-and-e-minor.md))
- **Chord diagram**: a picture of the neck showing where the fingers go for a chord. ([Chord diagrams and your first chord](../01-first-sounds/12-chord-diagrams-and-e-minor.md))
- **C position**: the right-hand shape with the thumb on C4 and fingers 1 to 5 on C, D, E, F and G. ([The treble staff and C position](../01-first-sounds/09-treble-staff-and-c-position.md))
- **Curved hand shape**: the fingers curved, the wrist level, as if holding a small ball. ([Sitting at the piano and finger numbers](../01-first-sounds/05-sitting-and-finger-numbers.md))

## D

- **Damper pedal**: the right pedal; it lets notes keep ringing. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))
- **Digital piano**: an electronic instrument that plays recorded piano sounds, usually with 88 weighted keys. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))
- **Double bar line**: two thin lines marking a new section. ([Bars, time signatures and rests](../01-first-sounds/08-bars-time-signatures-and-rests.md))
- **Down stroke**: moving the pick down across a string. ([Holding the guitar, tuning and the pick](../01-first-sounds/06-holding-tuning-and-the-pick.md), [Chord diagrams and your first chord](../01-first-sounds/12-chord-diagrams-and-e-minor.md))

## E

- **Em** (E minor): a chord with the notes E, G and B, played with fingers 2 and 3 on fret 2. ([Chord diagrams and your first chord](../01-first-sounds/12-chord-diagrams-and-e-minor.md))

## F

- **Finger numbers**: 1 (thumb) to 5 (little finger) on both hands. ([Sitting at the piano and finger numbers](../01-first-sounds/05-sitting-and-finger-numbers.md))
- **Flat** and **sharp** (as tuner words): too low and too high. ([Holding the guitar, tuning and the pick](../01-first-sounds/06-holding-tuning-and-the-pick.md))
- **Fret**: a metal bar across the fretboard. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Fretboard**: the front face of the neck. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Fret number**: the number on a tab line that says which fret to press; 0 is an open string. ([Reading guitar tab](../01-first-sounds/11-reading-tab.md))
- **Fretting**: pressing a string against the fretboard to change the note. ([Holding the guitar, tuning and the pick](../01-first-sounds/06-holding-tuning-and-the-pick.md))

## G

- **Gauge**: the thickness of a string. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Graded hammer action**: weighted keys that are heavier in the low part and lighter in the high part. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))

## H

- **Half note**: a note lasting 2 beats. ([Beat, tempo and note values](../01-first-sounds/07-beat-tempo-and-note-values.md))
- **Headstock**: the top end of the neck, holding the tuners. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))

## L

- **Ledger line**: a short extra line used for notes above or below the staff. ([The treble staff and C position](../01-first-sounds/09-treble-staff-and-c-position.md))
- **Let it ring**: leaving the chord to sound, with the fingers still down. ([Chord diagrams and your first chord](../01-first-sounds/12-chord-diagrams-and-e-minor.md))

## M

- **Melody**: a line of single notes that makes a tune. ([First melodies on piano](../01-first-sounds/10-first-melodies-on-piano.md))
- **Metronome**: a device or app that clicks at a steady speed. ([How to practise](../01-first-sounds/01-how-to-practise.md))
- **Middle C**: the C near the middle of the piano, written C4. ([Pitch and the musical alphabet](../01-first-sounds/04-pitch-and-the-musical-alphabet.md))
- **MIDI**: a standard for sending note messages between instruments and computers. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))
- **Musical alphabet**: the seven letters A to G, repeating. ([Pitch and the musical alphabet](../01-first-sounds/04-pitch-and-the-musical-alphabet.md))

## N

- **Neck**: the long arm that carries the fretboard. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Note head**: the oval part of a note. ([The treble staff and C position](../01-first-sounds/09-treble-staff-and-c-position.md))
- **Note value**: how many beats a note lasts. ([Beat, tempo and note values](../01-first-sounds/07-beat-tempo-and-note-values.md))
- **Nut**: the small bar where the strings leave the headstock. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))

## O

- **Octave**: the gap from one note to the next note with the same letter name. ([Pitch and the musical alphabet](../01-first-sounds/04-pitch-and-the-musical-alphabet.md))
- **Octave number**: the number after a note letter that says which octave it is in, such as the 4 in C4. ([Pitch and the musical alphabet](../01-first-sounds/04-pitch-and-the-musical-alphabet.md))
- **Open string**: a string played with no finger pressing it. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))

## P

- **Phrase**: a short musical idea within a melody, like a line of a sentence. ([First melodies on piano](../01-first-sounds/10-first-melodies-on-piano.md))
- **Pick** (plectrum): a small piece of plastic for sounding the strings. ([Holding the guitar, tuning and the pick](../01-first-sounds/06-holding-tuning-and-the-pick.md))
- **Pitch**: how high or low a note sounds. ([Pitch and the musical alphabet](../01-first-sounds/04-pitch-and-the-musical-alphabet.md))
- **Polyphony**: how many notes can sound at once. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))
- **Practice routine**: a timed plan for a session, with a warm-up, a new skill, reading or ear training, and time on the other instrument. ([How to practise](../01-first-sounds/01-how-to-practise.md))

## Q

- **Quarter note**: a note lasting 1 beat. ([Beat, tempo and note values](../01-first-sounds/07-beat-tempo-and-note-values.md))
- **Quarter rest** , **half rest**, **whole rest**: rests lasting 1 beat, 2 beats and a full bar. ([Bars, time signatures and rests](../01-first-sounds/08-bars-time-signatures-and-rests.md))

## R

- **Rest**: a silence with a set length in beats. ([Bars, time signatures and rests](../01-first-sounds/08-bars-time-signatures-and-rests.md))
- **RH** and **LH**: right hand and left hand. ([Sitting at the piano and finger numbers](../01-first-sounds/05-sitting-and-finger-numbers.md))

## S

- **Saddle**: the strip in the bridge that the strings rest on. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Sostenuto pedal**: the middle pedal; it holds only the notes already down. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))
- **Soundhole**: the round opening in the top. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Staff**: five horizontal lines and four spaces on which notes are written. ([The treble staff and C position](../01-first-sounds/09-treble-staff-and-c-position.md))
- **Strum**: sweeping the pick across several strings. ([Chord diagrams and your first chord](../01-first-sounds/12-chord-diagrams-and-e-minor.md))

## T

- **T, 1, 2, 3, 4**: the thumb and the four fingers of the fretting hand. ([Holding the guitar, tuning and the pick](../01-first-sounds/06-holding-tuning-and-the-pick.md))
- **Tab** (tablature): a six-line chart for guitar, one line per string, with numbers for frets. ([Reading guitar tab](../01-first-sounds/11-reading-tab.md))
- **Tempo**: the speed of the music. ([How to practise](../01-first-sounds/01-how-to-practise.md), [Beat, tempo and note values](../01-first-sounds/07-beat-tempo-and-note-values.md))
- **Three clean in a row**: playing a passage three times with no stops and no wrong notes; one mistake resets the count. ([How to practise](../01-first-sounds/01-how-to-practise.md))
- **Time signature**: two numbers at the start that say how many beats are in a bar and which note gets one beat. ([Bars, time signatures and rests](../01-first-sounds/08-bars-time-signatures-and-rests.md))
- **Top**: the front face of the body. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md))
- **Touch sensitivity**: the sound changes with how fast you press the key. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))
- **Traditional**: a tune from folk tradition, with no known composer, free to use. ([First melodies on piano](../01-first-sounds/10-first-melodies-on-piano.md))
- **Treble clef** (G clef): the sign that marks the second line from the bottom as G4. ([The treble staff and C position](../01-first-sounds/09-treble-staff-and-c-position.md))
- **Tuner** (machine head): a knob that tightens or loosens a string. ([Meet your guitar](../01-first-sounds/03-meet-your-guitar.md), [Holding the guitar, tuning and the pick](../01-first-sounds/06-holding-tuning-and-the-pick.md))

## U

- **Una corda pedal**: the left pedal; it makes the sound soft. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))

## V

- **Velocity**: how fast a key is pressed. ([Meet your piano](../01-first-sounds/02-meet-your-piano.md))

## W

- **Whole note**: a note lasting 4 beats. ([Beat, tempo and note values](../01-first-sounds/07-beat-tempo-and-note-values.md))
