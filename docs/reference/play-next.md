# Play next

Real film, game and anime music in books sold with the composer's or publisher's permission, matched to the lesson after which each one is within reach. This book can't print copyrighted music, so these are the books to buy when you want the real thing. The titles and publishers were checked in November 2026; editions change, so look at the publisher's page for the current one, and check that a book says it is licensed or authorized before you buy it.

The levels are rough. "Start after" is the earliest lesson that gives you the skills the easiest pieces in the book need; harder pieces in the same book can wait until later stages.

## Guitar

Official guitar books of film, game and anime music are fewer, and most are in tab with chord boxes. Look for books from the same publishers (Hal Leonard, Alfred Music, Yamaha Music Entertainment) whose cover or description says licensed or authorized. Chord-and-tab arrangements fit after [barre chord pieces](../08-fretboard-barre-chords-and-caged/04-barre-chord-pieces.md); fingerstyle solo arrangements fit after [Giuliani: right-hand studies](../10-classical-repertoire/02-giuliani-right-hand-studies.md) and [Travis picking](../07-pedal-texture-and-ambient/11-travis-picking.md).

## Working out music by ear

You can also learn film, game and anime music by ear for your own practice, with the method in [playing by ear](../13-capstones/08-playing-by-ear.md). Keep what you work out for yourself: copyrighted music you transcribe can't be shared or published without permission.

## How to learn a piece from one of these books

1. Look up the key and the time signature, and play the scale of the key once.
2. Mark the sections (usually 4 or 8 bars each) and the hard spots, as in the piece lessons in this book.
3. Learn the hard spots first, hands separately, slowly, then put the sections together.
4. Use your 30-minute routine: the new piece takes the 15-minute slot.
